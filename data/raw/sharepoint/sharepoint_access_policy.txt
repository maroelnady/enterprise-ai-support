Enterprise IT Services
SharePoint Access Policy

Document ID: IT-SP-001
Version: 1.0
Department: IT Services

1. Purpose

This policy defines how employees request access to SharePoint sites
and resources.

2. Access Request

Employees who require access to a SharePoint site must submit an
access request through the approved IT Service Desk process.

The request should include:

- Employee name.
- Department.
- SharePoint site name.
- Business justification.
- Manager approval when required.

3. Approval

Access to restricted SharePoint sites requires approval from the
appropriate site owner or authorized manager.

4. Access Provisioning

After approval, IT Services or the designated SharePoint administrator
grants the required permissions.

Employees should receive confirmation after access has been provisioned.

5. Security

Employees must only access information required for their job duties.

Employees must not share their account credentials with another person.

6. Support

If access is not available after approval, contact the IT Service Desk
and provide the SharePoint site name and error message.
