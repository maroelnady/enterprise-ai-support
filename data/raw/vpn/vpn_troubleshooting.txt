Enterprise IT Services
VPN Troubleshooting Guide

Document ID: IT-VPN-003
Version: 1.0
Department: IT Services

1. Authentication Failure

If the VPN reports an authentication failure:

- Confirm that the username is correct.
- Confirm that the VPN account is activated.
- Confirm that the password is correct.
- Complete the MFA request.

2. MFA Failure

If MFA does not work:

- Confirm that the registered authentication device is available.
- Check whether the MFA notification was received.
- Do not repeatedly approve unexpected MFA requests.
- Contact the IT Service Desk if MFA remains unavailable.

3. Connection Failure

If the VPN cannot establish a connection:

- Verify internet connectivity.
- Restart the VPN client.
- Confirm that the VPN server address is correct.
- Try connecting again.

4. Escalation

If troubleshooting does not resolve the issue, contact the IT Service Desk.

Provide:

- Username
- Error message
- Approximate time of failure
- Screenshot of the error if available

Never provide your password or MFA verification code to the Service Desk.
