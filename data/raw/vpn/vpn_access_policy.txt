Enterprise IT Services
VPN Access Policy

Document ID: IT-VPN-001
Version: 1.0
Department: IT Services

1. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for employees who need secure
remote access to Enterprise IT Services systems.

2. Eligibility

VPN access is available to employees who require remote access to
authorized company systems.

3. Approval

Employees must obtain manager approval before VPN access can be
activated.

4. Security Requirements

All VPN users must:

- Use company-managed devices.
- Use multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Protect their VPN credentials.
- Never share VPN credentials with another person.

5. Access Request

The employee must submit a VPN access request through the IT Service
Desk.

The request must include:

- Employee name
- Department
- Manager approval
- Business justification

6. Activation

After approval, the IT Service Desk activates the VPN account.
The employee receives configuration instructions after activation.

7. Security Restrictions

VPN credentials must not be shared.

Users must disconnect from VPN when remote access is no longer required.

8. Support

If VPN access does not work after activation, the employee should
contact the IT Service Desk and provide the error message.

End of Document
