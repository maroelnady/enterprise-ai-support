Enterprise IT Services
VPN Setup Guide

Document ID: IT-VPN-002
Version: 1.0
Department: IT Services

1. Prerequisites

Before configuring the VPN client, confirm that:

- VPN access has been approved.
- The VPN account has been activated.
- Multi-factor authentication has been registered.
- The device is connected to the internet.

2. VPN Client

Install the approved Enterprise VPN client on the company-managed device.

3. Configuration

Open the VPN client and enter the server address provided by IT Services.

Enter your company username and password.

Complete the MFA verification when prompted.

4. Connection

Select Connect.

A successful connection indicates that the device has established
secure remote access.

5. Troubleshooting

If the connection fails:

- Confirm that your internet connection is working.
- Verify your username.
- Verify that MFA is working.
- Confirm that your VPN account has been activated.

If the problem continues, contact the IT Service Desk.
