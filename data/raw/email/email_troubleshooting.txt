Enterprise IT Services
Email Troubleshooting Guide

Document ID: IT-EML-002
Version: 1.0
Department: IT Services

1. Purpose

This guide provides troubleshooting steps for common company email
problems.

2. Cannot Sign In

If the employee cannot sign in:

- Verify the company email address.
- Verify that the account is active.
- Confirm that the current password is being used.
- Complete MFA verification if requested.

3. Email Not Synchronizing

If new messages are not appearing:

- Verify internet connectivity.
- Restart the email application.
- Confirm that the account is connected.
- Check whether the mailbox is full.

4. Suspicious Email

Employees must not open suspicious links or attachments.

If a suspicious email is received, report it using the approved
security reporting process.

5. Escalation

If the issue continues, contact the IT Service Desk.

Provide:

- Email address.
- Error message.
- Approximate time the problem occurred.

Never provide a password or MFA verification code.
