Enterprise IT Services
Email Setup Guide

Document ID: IT-EML-001
Version: 1.0
Department: IT Services

1. Purpose

This guide explains how employees can configure their company email
on an approved device.

2. Prerequisites

Before configuring email, the employee must have:

- An active company account.
- An assigned company email address.
- Internet connectivity.
- MFA registration where required.

3. Email Configuration

Employees should use the approved Enterprise Email application.

When prompted:

- Enter the company email address.
- Enter the company password.
- Complete MFA verification if requested.

The application should automatically retrieve the approved email
configuration.

4. Security

Employees must:

- Use only approved email applications.
- Protect their company credentials.
- Never share passwords or MFA codes.
- Report suspicious email activity to IT Security.

5. Support

If email configuration fails, the employee should contact the
IT Service Desk and provide the error message.
